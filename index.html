Observable Bodily Manifestations

Hippocratic Principle of Contraries (Contraria Contrariis Curantur): Diseases caused by excess heat or moisture are treated with opposite dietetics, herbal purges, or surgical evacuation to restore natural equilibrium.

Hippocratic Clinic: Patient Case Files

Case I: Senator Marcus of Athens Acute Crisis

Presenting with intense rage, a red burning face, violent headache, and bitter taste in the mouth after a heavy feast in midsummer.

Case II: Lysandra the Scribe Chronic Malady

Exhibits deep melancholia, cold hands, dark eye rings, digestive discomfort, and refusal to sleep or speak during autumn.

Case III: Captain Theron of Corinth Winter Affliction

Complains of heavy phlegm in lungs, swelling in ankles (dropsy), severe drowsiness, apathy, and cold clammy skin.
